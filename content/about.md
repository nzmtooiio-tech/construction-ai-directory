## Why This Directory Exists

Construction is the world's largest industry — and one of the last to fully adopt AI. **Contractor AI Toolkit** was built because finding the right AI tool for a job site shouldn't require 10 hours of research.

We list, test, and categorize AI tools specifically built for construction workflows — from takeoff to closeout.

## How We Evaluate Tools

Every tool in the directory is assessed on:

- **AI genuineness** — Is AI core to the product, or just a marketing label?
- **Construction fit** — Does it solve a real job-site problem?
- **Integration depth** — Does it connect with Procore, Autodesk, or common ERP systems?
- **Pricing transparency** — Can you evaluate cost without a sales call?
- **Active development** — Is the product being maintained and improved?

## Featured Listings

Some tools appear as **Featured** — this means the vendor has paid for premium placement. Featured status does not affect our evaluation scores.
